TNB TITLE SEARCH SERVICES — STATIC HTML EDITION

Same updated design as the PHP edition, with all 17 pages as HTML.
Includes responsive mega menus, image-led sections, service and process tabs,
FAQs, testimonial layout, and the video slider.

GET STARTED
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in your browser.
No PHP, Node.js, database, build command, or backend is required.
For hosting, upload the contents of this folder to your web root.

LOCAL ASSETS
assets/images/ — 9 photos and SVG favicon
assets/videos/ — 3 MP4 clips
assets/fonts/ — local fonts and licenses
assets/style.css — shared styling
assets/app.js — browser interactions
assets/media-manifest.json — asset source information

EDITING
Edit the individual .html files, assets/style.css, and assets/app.js.
Navigation and footer markup is repeated across the HTML pages;
apply shared edits consistently across pages.

FORM AND CONTENT
The contact form downloads an inquiry draft locally. It does not send or store data.
Testimonials are clearly labelled illustrative samples.
Stock media does not depict TNB premises or staff.
The three video files use a property walkthrough and two edited excerpts.
Verified business contact details and final policies still need client input.
