05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Here’s what to know before we get started.

Have another question? ↗
What title search services do you offer?

Full Title Search, Two-Owner Search (2OS), Current Owner Search (COS), quality control, property-record research, and dedicated team support, based on agreed client requirements.

Which US states and counties do you cover?

Coverage depends on team capability, authorized source access, and your product requirements. Ask us for the current verified coverage list for your assignment.

Can you support recurring or overflow work?

Yes, we can discuss recurring and overflow assignments. Capacity, staffing, volume, and delivery expectations are agreed before work begins.

How are pricing and turnaround determined?

We review the product, jurisdiction, search period, complexity, source access, and volume to agree on pricing and turnaround for your requirements.

How is client information handled?

Confidentiality requirements, secure transfer methods, access controls, and retention procedures are confirmed with each client during onboarding.

Do you provide title insurance or legal opinions?

TNB provides title research and operational support. Our services are not title insurance or legal opinions.
