Scope-led research.
Client-specific delivery.

Research centered on current vesting and requested record items. The assignment is tailored to the property, product, jurisdiction, and available authorized sources.

What the scope may include

  • Current owner and vesting documents
  • Relevant recorded deeds
  • Mortgage and release information
  • Requested liens, judgments, and tax details

Before we begin

We confirm your search period, required records, report template, source access, delivery expectations, and escalation procedure. The precise scope is agreed before work starts.

Built for your workflow

Share your template, product checklist, and vendor onboarding requirements. We’ll discuss a suitable approach and identify any scope or coverage limitations.
